These files are those annotated following the "method B" annotation protocol. 

This protocol is largely made up of the fact it is only paying attention to two things: 

- the larger musical sections on the page
- the actual chant texts

You can refer to this sample image in the mothra discussion thread:
https://github.com/DDMAL/mothra/discussions/5#discussioncomment-16935923

Note how the text are all included in one box, and the decorated initial is excluded. 
Additionally, note how the rubric is also excluded: text that is not beneath music is excluded. 

Marginalia are excluded, unless there's music, in which case follow the annotation protocol (even if there are no stafflines)
